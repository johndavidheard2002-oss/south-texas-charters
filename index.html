Before you book

What do trips cost?
4-hour trips are $650 for 2 anglers (+$75 each additional). 6-hour trips are $700 for 2 anglers (+$100 each additional). Call 281-785-3334 to reserve.
Where do you fish?
Bay fishing around Aransas Pass and the South Texas Coast — Port Aransas, Ingleside, and nearby flats and channels depending on tides and conditions.
Do you do fish mounts?
Yes — full and half fiberglass mounts with 45+ years of experience. Mounts are secondary to charters; ask when you call so we can plan sizing and timing.
What is Tide Mark?
Tide Mark is our fishing logbook app for saltwater catches, tides, and spots. South Texas Charters is the home of Tide Mark.
How do I book?
Call 281-785-3334 or email FishAransasPass@gmail.com. We’ll confirm dates, party size, and trip length.
