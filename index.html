Before you book

What do trips cost?
4-hour trips are $650 for 2 anglers (+$75 each additional). 6-hour trips are $700 for 2 anglers (+$100 each additional).
Where do you fish?
Bay fishing around Aransas Pass and the South Texas Coast — Port Aransas, Ingleside, and nearby flats and channels depending on tides and conditions.
Do you do fish mounts?
Yes — full and half fiberglass mounts with 45+ years of experience. Ask when you call so we can plan sizing and timing.
How do I book?
Call 281-785-3334 or email FishAransasPass@gmail.com. We’ll confirm dates, party size, and trip length.
